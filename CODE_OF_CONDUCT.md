# Code of conduct

Tempolight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people new to LEDs and to code, and keep criticism about
the work rather than the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated anywhere in the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/D4RKGEEK). Reports are handled in
confidence.
