A short tour of everything in Tempolight, one feature at a time. Each part says what it does and where to find it.
The menu is on the left on a computer and at the bottom on a phone. A path like **Music → In sync** means: open
**Music** in the menu, then look for the box called *In sync*.

New here? Start with [Your first show](/guides/first-run), or let [Claude set it up for you](/setup-with-claude).

## Play a song

Tempolight listens to the whole song first, so it knows every beat, build-up and drop before they come.

- **Music → Play songs → Add songs…** and pick songs from your computer (MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC).
- **Music → Play songs → Play the demo track** to try it with a song that comes with Tempolight.

Your songs stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

## Music you play somewhere else

Spotify, YouTube, a DJ app or a speaker in the room work too. Tempolight listens and follows along.

- **Music → How should Tempolight hear your music?** walks you through it. Pick *This computer's sound* or *The
  microphone*.
- **Music → Listen live → Listen to this computer's sound** or **Listen with a microphone** starts listening right
  away.
- On a Mac, the computer's sound needs a small free tool called BlackHole. The panel shows each step.
  [Hearing the music](/hearing-music) has the details.

## Spotify

The lights follow the Spotify song with its full beat map. Tempolight learns each song the first time it plays.

- **Music → Sync with Spotify**, then tick **Sync with Spotify**.
- More in [Sync with Spotify](/spotify).

## Your own music library

Add your music folder and Tempolight studies every song in it ahead of time. Then each song is in sync from its very
first play, whichever player you use.

- **Music → Your own music → Add a folder**: type the folder's full path, for example `/home/you/Music`, then press
  **Add**.
- **Look for new songs** checks the folder again. **Pause** stops the work for now.
- Most music files need a free tool called ffmpeg. WAV files work without it.
- More in [Your own music](/guides/your-music).

## Songs it remembers

Every song you play in Tempolight is remembered. When it plays again from anywhere, Tempolight knows it after about
8 seconds of listening, even if the song is a little faster or slower or the sound is different.

- **Music → Song memory** lists the songs it knows. Tick **On** to use it.

## Is it in sync

A meter tells you how well the lights follow the beat.

- **Music → In sync**.
- *Locked* means on the beat. *Finding the beat* means wait a few seconds. *Not hearing* means nothing to follow.
- The line under it tells you why, or what to do. For example, *Following the song's beat map* means it knows the
  song and every beat in it.
- **Home** shows the same as a small dot before the status line. Green is locked.

## The song map

A picture of the song that is playing: how loud it is, its parts and its drops.

- **Music → In sync**, under the meter.
- Blue is calm, amber is a build-up, pink is a drop. Parts that repeat get the same letter.
- The dashed line is the next drop. The line under the map counts down to it.

## One look per song

Tempolight picks one effect and one set of colours for each song, to fit it. Fast dance music gets an effect that
hits on every kick. A slow song gets a calm one. The same song always gets the same look.

- **Music → In sync → Look** shows the look, for example *Kick Shockwave · Sunset*.
- **Not this one** picks the next best look. The old one won't come back for this song.
- **Keep** keeps this look for this song.

## More or less change

Choose how often the effect changes.

- **Effects → How effects are chosen → Variety**.
- *One look per song*: one effect for the whole song. This is the default.
- *Song parts*: one effect for calm parts, one for build-ups, one for drops.
- *Show (changes often)*: the effect changes with the song, every few bars.

## Pick an effect yourself

There are 95 effects. Automatic mode picks them for you, from effects tested on drops, build-ups and calm parts.

- **Effects**, then tap an effect to show it. **Back to automatic** lets Tempolight choose again.
- The heart on an effect means automatic mode picks it more often. The ban sign means it never picks it.
- **Effects → Effect settings** changes the speed, intensity and colours of one effect.

## Make your own effect

Build an effect from simple parts, without code, and share it with friends.

- **Effects → Make your own**. Pick a pattern, a direction, what moves it and the colours.

## How hard it hits

Two sliders set the feel of the show.

- **Settings → Lighting → Punch**: how hard the whole room pumps on each kick in a drop.
- **Settings → Lighting → Drama**: how much brighter drops are than calm parts, with a short dip just before each
  drop.
- **Settings → Lighting → Vary drops every 2 bars** flips the colours or the direction during drops.

## All lights flash together

Some lights are slower than others. Hue and WiZ bulbs take about 100 ms to change, WLED about 20 ms. Tempolight works
out the delay of each light by itself and holds the faster ones back, so a kick flashes on all of them at once.

- Nothing to set. **Devices → Sound to light** shows each light's delay.
- If your speakers play late (Bluetooth does), use **Music → Timing → Fix the timing automatically** once.

## Drop countdown

Tempolight hears a drop coming a few seconds ahead, even in a song it has never heard. The lights build up to it by
themselves: brighter and faster, a short dip, then the flash.

- **Live pads** shows a big *Drop in 4 · 3 · 2 · 1* just before the drop.
- Turn it off in **Settings → Lighting → Show drop countdown**.

## Play the lights live

Big buttons for a flash, a strobe, a blackout, a build-up and a drop, on top of the show.

- **Live pads**. Keys work too: Space is a flash, B a blackout, U a build-up and drop.
- **Live pads → MIDI controller** connects a pad controller.
- More in [Parties and DJs](/guides/party-and-dj).

## Chord colours

The colour changes when the chord changes, on the start of a bar. A chorus that comes back gets the same colours.

- **Settings → Lighting → Chord colours**. It works when the colours are set to *Automatic*.

## Stereo

When the sound comes in stereo, the side of the room a hit came from lights up more.

- **Settings → Lighting → Stereo**: *Off*, *Subtle* (the default) or *Strong*.
- A microphone is mono, so nothing changes with it.

## Your room

Tell Tempolight where your lights are, so effects move across the room the way it really is.

- **Room → Start from a layout** picks a ready-made room.
- **Room → Map with the camera…** lets a phone camera find where every LED is.
- **Room → Zones** gives part of the room its own effect or brightness.
- More in [Your room and zones](/guides/room-and-zones).

## Add lights

- **Devices → Scan network** finds lights on your Wi-Fi.
- **Devices → Add a controller** adds one by its address.
- Which lights work, and how: [Connect your lights](/guides/connect-lights).

## Scenes and schedules

A scene saves the effect, colours and brightness. A schedule turns a scene on at a set time.

- **Home → Save as scene** saves what is showing.
- **Scenes & schedules** lists your scenes and sets times, including sunrise and sunset.

## Wake up and fall asleep

- **Home & sleep → Wake-up sunrise**: the room gets lighter before your alarm.
- **Home & sleep → Right now → Sleep in 30 min** fades the lights out. Tap 15, 30, 45 or 60 first to pick how long.
- **Home & sleep → Ambient plan**: a soft light for each hour when no music plays.
- **Settings → Night mode and location**: dimmer, warmer light at night, with no strobes.
- More in [Wake-up and sleep](/wake-up).

## Parties

- **Live pads → Phones as lights**: guests scan a QR code and their phone screen joins the show.
- **Music → Guest requests**: guests ask for songs from their phones and vote.
- **Settings → Lock**: a PIN keeps guests out of your settings.
- **Home → Record clip** records 15 seconds of the show to share.

## Screen sync

Share a film, a game or a music video and the room takes its colours.

- **Music → Screen sync**.

## Smart home

- **Settings → Home Assistant** connects to Home Assistant.
- **Settings → Apple Home** lets Siri and the Home app control Tempolight.
- More in [Smart home](/guides/smart-home) and [Voice control](/voice).

## Streams and games

- **Integrations**: Stream Deck, OBS, CS2 and Dota 2, Twitch and Discord.
- More in [Streamers and gamers](/guides/creators).

## Safe for everyone

- **Settings → Lighting → Photosensitive-safe mode**: at most 3 flashes a second, over everything.

## Keep it up to date

- **Settings → About → Check for updates**.
- **Settings → Backup → Download backup** saves your lights, room, scenes and settings in one file.
- **Settings → About → Run setup again** opens the setup wizard.
