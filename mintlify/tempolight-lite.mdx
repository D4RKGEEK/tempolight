**Beta.** The analysis is tested on a computer with synthetic songs; it hasn't been tuned in real rooms yet (see
[Status](#status)).

Tempolight normally runs on a computer or a Raspberry Pi: it listens to the music, works out the beat, the build-ups
and the drops, and streams every pixel to your WLED controllers. **Tempolight Lite** puts a small version of that
listening on the controller itself. An ESP32 running WLED, with a cheap I2S microphone (an INMP441 costs a couple of
euros), hears the room and drives its own LEDs with Tempolight effects. Nothing else has to be switched on.

It's a WLED usermod. It doesn't replace WLED or its AudioReactive usermod: AudioReactive still drives the
microphone and computes the spectrum, and Lite reads that spectrum and adds the Tempolight part.

## What Lite can do, and what needs the full app

| | Tempolight Lite (on the ESP32) | Tempolight (computer or Pi) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kicks and snares | Yes, from WLED's 16 frequency channels (43 Hz to 9 kHz, a new frame every 23 ms) | Yes, from its own FFT (11.6 ms hops), plus hi-hats |
| Tempo and beat grid | Yes, 60 to 180 BPM, locked to the kicks; bars start on the drop | Yes, with half/double-time checks and song style |
| Song part | Calm, build-up, drop, silent. Lite notices a drop about a beat after it starts | Same live; with a song file it knows where every drop is before it plays |
| Build-up countdown to the exact drop | No: Lite shows how long the build-up has been running | Yes, with song files and beat maps |
| Effects | 9 (below), on the segment's palette | over 90 ([effects.md](/effects)), across a whole room layout |
| Several controllers as one show | No: each Lite controller listens on its own. You can share one microphone with WLED's audio sync | Yes, one show over every controller, Hue, DMX, Nanoleaf and more |
| Key, mood colours, vocals, song memory, styles | No | Yes |
| Scenes, schedules, live pads, phone remote, Home Assistant | WLED's own presets, playlists, schedules and app | Yes |
| Photosensitivity limit | Yes, at most 3 flashes a second over all segments, and a "No flashes" switch | Yes |
| Hardware | Classic ESP32 (dual core) with an I2S microphone | Any computer, a Pi, or a phone as the microphone |

Lite and the full app work well together. When Tempolight streams to the controller (DDP), WLED shows the stream; when
the stream stops, WLED goes back to its own effect, so a controller left on **TL Auto** is music-reactive on its own
again a few seconds after the computer goes away. Tempolight can also send WLED audio sync packets (the 44-byte "V2"
packet in [protocols.md](/protocols)); a Lite controller set to receive audio sync analyses those instead of its
microphone.

## The effects

All of them follow the music; pick them in WLED like any other effect (they're listed with the audio effects, names
start with **TL**). Colours come from the segment's palette.

| Effect | What it does | Sliders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TL Auto | Picks one of the others from the song part: Aurora when calm (Tempo Wave every other 8 bars when there's a beat), Build Fill in a build-up, Drop Strobe for the first 8 bars of a drop, then Kick Shockwave, Bass Rain, Beat Chase and Bar Pulse, changing every 8 bars | Speed, Intensity, No flashes |
| TL Kick Shockwave | A ring flies out from the centre on every kick | Speed, Ring width |
| TL Beat Chase | A comet that lands on the next quarter of the strip on every beat of the bar | Speed (without tempo), Tail |
| TL Build Fill | Build-ups fill the strip from both ends towards the middle, flickering faster as they rise; bursts at the drop | |
| TL Drop Strobe | White flashes when the drop hits and on kicks during it, never more than 3 a second; beat pulses in between | No flashes |
| TL Aurora | Slow drifting colour waves that breathe with the loudness | Drift, Brightness |
| TL Bass Rain | Drops fall along the strip on kicks; louder bass, longer drops | Fall speed, Fade |
| TL Bar Pulse | A bar from the centre that grows with the bass and kicks, a new colour every bar | Bass gain |
| TL Tempo Wave | A colour wave that travels one strip length per bar, brightened by the kicks | Waves |

The effects are written for strips (1D). On a 2D matrix, WLED's segment mapping options ("Expand 1D FX": bar, arc,
corner, pinwheel) spread them over the panel.

## What you need

- A classic ESP32 board (ESP32-WROOM, "esp32dev", 4 MB flash). ESP32-S3 should work but isn't tested yet; ESP32-S2,
  ESP32-C3 and ESP8266 are too slow for AudioReactive.
- An I2S MEMS microphone: INMP441 (or ICS-43434, SPH0645, any "generic I2S" microphone).
- WS2812B / SK6812 LEDs and a power supply sized for them, as for any WLED build.

## Wiring the INMP441

| INMP441 | ESP32 |
| --- | --- |
| VDD | 3V3 (not 5 V) |
| GND | GND |
| L/R | GND (the microphone sends on the left channel) |
| WS | GPIO 15 |
| SCK | GPIO 14 |
| SD | GPIO 32 |

These are AudioReactive's defaults; the Lite firmware sets the microphone type to "Generic I2S" with them. LED data
goes on GPIO 16, WLED's default. Keep the microphone wires short (under 20 cm), put the microphone where it hears the
speakers rather than the LED power supply, and don't point the hole at a wall.

## Flashing

**From the browser:** open the [installer page](https://d4rkgeek.github.io/tempolight/flash/) in Chrome or Edge on a computer, plug the ESP32
in with a USB data cable and press Install. It erases the board and installs WLED with AudioReactive and Tempolight
Lite (a factory image from the latest release). The installer may offer to set up Wi-Fi right after flashing (WLED
speaks Improv over USB); otherwise join the `WLED-AP` network (password `wled1234`) and follow WLED's setup.

**Updating a controller that already runs WLED 16:** download `tempolight-lite-esp32-update.bin` from the release and
upload it in WLED: Config > Security & Updates > Manual OTA update. Your settings stay. (A build without
AudioReactive needs the microphone set up afterwards in Config > Usermods.)

**Building it yourself:** see [firmware/README.md](https://github.com/D4RKGEEK/tempolight/blob/main/firmware/README.md). In short, clone WLED 16, run
`firmware/scripts/add-to-wled.sh path/to/WLED`, then `npm ci && pio run -e esp32dev_tempolight` in the WLED folder.

## Setting it up

1. **Microphone:** Config > Usermods > AudioReactive: enabled, Generic I2S, the pins above. Play music and open the
   GEQ effect: the low bars should jump on the kick.
2. **Quiet room = dark:** raise AudioReactive's Squelch until the GEQ is empty with no music, and set AGC to Normal.
   Leave "FFT scaling" on Square root (the default); if you change it, set the same in Tempolight's settings below.
3. **LEDs:** Config > LED Preferences, as usual.
4. **Effect:** choose **TL Auto** on a segment (or any TL effect). Info shows **tempolight** with the tempo and song part
   and an on/off button.

Settings (Config > Usermods > tempolight):

| Setting | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| enabled | on | Analysis on or off. Off: TL effects show their quiet look |
| sensitivity | 128 | Higher finds quieter kicks (0 to 255). Lower it if hi-hats or bass notes count as kicks |
| fftScale | 3 | Must match AudioReactive's FFT scaling (0 none, 1 log, 2 linear, 3 square root) |
| latencyMs | 20 | How far the beat grid is moved earlier to make up for the microphone frame; add a little if lights look late |
| strobe | on | Allow white flashes at all |
| maxFlashes | 3 | Flashes per second over all segments; it can be lowered, never raised above 3 |

## For apps: the JSON API

`GET /json/info` on a Lite controller contains:

```json
"tempolight": { "lite": "0.1.0", "on": true, "audio": true,
  "fx": { "shockwave": 186, "chase": 187, "build": 188, "strobe": 189, "aurora": 190, "rain": 191, "pulse": 192, "wave": 193, "auto": 194 } }
```

`lite` is the usermod version (its presence marks a Lite device), `audio` says whether AudioReactive is delivering
data, and `fx` gives the effect ids, which WLED assigns at boot (they depend on the build). `GET /json/state`
contains the live analysis:

```json
"tempolight": { "on": true, "bpm": 128, "locked": true, "section": "drop", "beat": 1, "drops": 1, "build": 0 }
```

`POST /json/state` with `{"tempolight":{"on":false}}` switches the analysis off (`true` on, `"t"` toggles), and
`{"tempolight":{"auto":true}}` puts the selected segments (or the main segment) on TL Auto. Since `on` is part of the
state, WLED presets can switch Lite on and off too.

## Status

The analysis and the effects are tested on a computer with synthetic songs (128, 100, 120, 132, 140 and 150 BPM, with
and without microphone noise) passed through an emulation of AudioReactive's spectrum: tempo within 0.2 BPM, the beat grid
within 6 ms of the real beats on average, the drop found within two beats of its start, no false drops in the
build-up, silence and drumless music staying calm or silent, and never more than 3 flashes a second. Real rooms, real
microphones and real music are the next step: thresholds may need tuning once it has been heard on hardware.
