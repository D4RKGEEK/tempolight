<h2 className="tl-h2">Everything Tempolight does</h2>
<p className="tl-sub">One app, from the music in to the light out.</p>

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Song analysis" icon="audio-waveform" href="/guides/music-sources">
    Songs are analysed before they play: beat grid, song parts, drops, key and mood.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live listening" icon="ear" href="/hearing-music">
    A microphone, the computer's sound or a sound card on a Pi, with songs recognised as they play.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Effects and the show editor" icon="wand-sparkles" href="/effects">
    95 effects on the real shape of your room, chosen by a director that follows the song.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Devices" icon="lightbulb" href="/guides/connect-lights">
    Found on your network by themselves, with zones, power limits and a latency meter.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Spotify sync" icon="disc-3" href="/spotify">
    Exact sync once a song is learned, songs learned on their first play, shared beat maps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Smart home" icon="house" href="/guides/smart-home">
    Home Assistant, Apple Home, Google Assistant, Alexa, wake-up sunrises and night mode.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DJs and parties" icon="party-popper" href="/guides/party-and-dj">
    Live pads, MIDI, Pro DJ Link, Ableton Link, scenes, guest requests and a safe mode.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creators" icon="video" href="/guides/creators">
    Stream Deck, OBS, game events, Twitch and Discord.
  </Card>
  <Card title="API" icon="braces" href="/api">
    Everything the app does, over plain HTTP and one WebSocket.
  </Card>
